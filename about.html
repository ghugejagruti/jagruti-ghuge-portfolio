Frame 01 way of interest

Who I am.

I'm Jagruti, a UX design student based in Pune. As an ISTP, I'm naturally analytical, independent, and hands-on, I'd rather test something myself than take someone's word for it, and I default to practical, logical decision-making over going with a gut feeling alone.

I'm comfortable in ambiguity, whether that's a startup brief that shifts weekly or a technical domain I have to learn from scratch. The work I care most about sits where complexity meets clarity: things that are genuinely hard to build but feel simple to use.

Frame 02

How I work.

01

Context before craft

I spend more time understanding why a problem exists than jumping into wireframes. The right screen for the wrong problem is just expensive polish.

02

Clarity over cleverness

A flow that everyone understands beats a clever one that only I can explain. If a user hesitates, that's a design problem, not a user problem.

03

Test, learn, redesign

A rough prototype in front of real users today beats a polished one in six months. The feedback is the most valuable thing I get from any project.
