01The challenge

A complex product, unfamiliar to most investors.

Propenize combines real estate, investing, KYC, escrow, wallets and tokenization into one experience.

My challenge was to turn that complexity into an interface that felt familiar, understandable and trustworthy.

08Reflection

What I learned

Designing Propenize taught me that a financial product can't be designed from screens alone. I had to understand the system behind the interface how ownership, money, compliance and investment decisions connect.
